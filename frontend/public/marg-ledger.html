    How to read this

    • Expected rate is the citywide failure rate for the same repair methods, in wards with the same flood-proneness, as this contractor's jobs.
    • Range is the 95% uncertainty band. Fewer jobs means a wider range, so a small contractor is not judged on a few jobs.
    • Flood-prone means a ward low-lying index of 0.6 or more.
    • Warranty bills are today's status from the dataset, so treat them as an upper bound.
    • There are no grades. The figures are for review and do not establish fault.
